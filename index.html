CUSTOMER DEPOSITS & PART-EXCHANGE RULES

A customer enquiry or viewing is not a reservation. A £250 deposit is held separately from business cash until collection; only the remaining balance is collected at handover. Buyer cancellation may allow fair retention for actual customer-specific costs, while dealership fault requires a full refund. Part-exchanges require an agreed allowance and occupy a stock space. No retail sale or revenue is recorded until handover.

FINANCIAL CONTROL.

Plan purchases, repairs and the cash needed to keep trading.

HOW TO READ THE FINANCES

Cash balance is actual dealership cash. Cash after repair reserves removes funds earmarked for outstanding vehicle preparation, but not future rent, wages or auction invoices. Stock invested is money already spent on vehicles still held; it cannot be used until a vehicle sells. Gross trade margin reflects completed deals, while operating contribution also deducts recorded staff costs, premises expenses, stock ageing and paid aftersales claims. Customer deposits are held separately. The cash forecast assumes no future sales and is planning-only; it does not charge cash. This is a fictional management view, not statutory accounts.
